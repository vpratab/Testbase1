# Partner Outreach Templates

These are draft messages only. They should be sent by an authorized company
representative after confirming export-control, proprietary-data, and proposal
rules.

## NP002 test-site or sensor-data partner

**Subject:** Phase I C-UAS sensing and track-custody validation collaboration

We are preparing a Navy Phase I proposal for a low-cost defensive C-UAS
sensing, identification, and track-custody module. The current prototype
includes a compact native runtime, authenticated interfaces, public acoustic
data evaluation, multimodal fusion surrogates, and dense-crossing tests.

We are seeking a partner able to provide either an instrumented controlled
collection or synchronized recorded radar, EO/IR, RF, acoustic, Remote ID, and
truth data for Group 1/2-or-below aircraft. We do not require defeat-system
control, sensitive engagement logic, or production access.

The requested collaboration is:

- a short technical call to confirm available modalities and truth;
- a letter describing the intended Phase I data/test path;
- if awarded, a bounded replay or collection with agreed metrics;
- optional participation in an operator/SME review.

Our contribution is the integration adapter, reproducible evaluation harness,
custody-aware tracking, uncertainty reporting, and evidence package.

## NV062 commercial tasking provider

**Subject:** Phase I provider-neutral secure tasking sandbox integration

We are preparing a Navy Phase I proposal for a government-controlled,
provider-neutral commercial-asset tasking gateway. The current prototype
validates public provider schemas, exercises task lifecycle and negative
security cases, verifies real open-data returns, and preserves signed
transaction evidence.

We are requesting:

- sandbox/developer credential guidance;
- confirmation of representative task, approval, cancellation, status, and
  return schemas;
- permission to identify the provider as a proposed Phase I integration path;
- no paid collection or production ordering authority during the initial
  feasibility work.

The integration does not replace provider security. It adds government-owned
purpose, release, approval, replay, retention, and cross-provider evidence.

## QSPARX cyber-range or mission-system advisor

**Subject:** Phase I cryptographic inventory and migration-range collaboration

We are preparing an Air Force Phase I proposal for dependency-aware
cryptographic inventory and post-quantum migration planning. The current
prototype discovers multiple cryptographic artifact classes, performs real
ML-KEM/ML-DSA operations, models dependency-safe migration waves, and preserves
signed rollback checkpoints.

We are seeking:

- a sanitized inventory schema or sponsor-approved cyber-range replica;
- representative dependency, key-storage, legacy-interoperability, and policy
  cases;
- an advisor able to review inventory coverage and migration disruption
  metrics;
- no private keys, mission data, or production write access.

## Thirty-minute call agenda

1. Confirm the exact Phase I capability boundary.
2. Identify available interface/data fields and restrictions.
3. Agree on the smallest useful Base-period demonstration.
4. Define measurable success and failure criteria.
5. Confirm proposal naming and letter-of-support constraints.
6. Record the path to representative Option or Phase II testing.
