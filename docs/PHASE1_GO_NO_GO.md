## Scoring rubric

The independent score uses:

- solicitation fit: 25 points;
- executable feasibility evidence: 25 points;
- representative data/environment: 20 points;
- baselines, stress, and failure evidence: 15 points;
- integration and transition credibility: 10 points;
- claim discipline and reproducibility: 5 points.

Internal 95+ requirement-coverage scores are engineering checks and must not
appear as award-likelihood claims.

## What is now proven

- The native execution path is fast and independently repeatable.
- Authenticated interfaces, replay rejection, and C/C++ integration work.
- Custody-aware sparse assignment materially improves deliberately ambiguous
  crossings without using truth identity in the assignment cost.
- Forecast parameters selected in Puget Sound transferred to a separate New
  York date and region and beat hold and raw-velocity baselines.
- Single-tier maritime anomaly alerting did not transfer cleanly. The failure
  is preserved, and a two-tier watch/high-confidence contract is supported.
- Every topic has explicit failure conditions and a reproducible evidence path.

## Submission rule

Submit the four GO topics first. Submit CONDITIONAL GO topics only when the
technical lead and first-month data-access plan are named. Submit PARTNER GO
topics only when the proposal identifies a real integration or data partner,
even if final credentials arrive after award.

No synthetic score should be used to claim these external blockers have been
solved.
