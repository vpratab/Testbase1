## Technical-volume rules

1. Lead with the customer performance question, not PZDR or RTVLAS branding.
2. State the baseline and exact improvement method.
3. Separate measured facts, modeled results, engineering gates, and Phase II
   targets.
4. Show a failure table and what the system does when uncertainty is excessive.
5. Include a one-page requirement-to-evidence traceability matrix.
6. Use one architecture diagram and one experiment diagram; eliminate generic
   AI imagery.
7. Include target-hardware, representative-environment, cybersecurity,
   data-rights, and transition work packages.
8. Name the government-furnished information needed during the first month.
9. Do not use internal 95+ scores in the technical abstract.
10. Make the Phase I demonstration independently reproducible.

## Reviewer questions that must be answered

- What is technically new relative to published work and existing products?
- Why is the team uniquely able to execute it?
- What exact experiment could prove the central hypothesis wrong?
- What data and environment are required from the sponsor?
- What runs in real time and on what hardware?
- How are uncertainty, false alarms, degraded operation, and operator authority
  handled?
- What is delivered at the end of Phase I?
- What program, platform, interface, and budget path could transition it?
- What commercial or dual-use market supports sustainment?
